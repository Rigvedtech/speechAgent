# Prabhat

Prabhat is an AI interview platform by Rigved Technologies for hiring teams. 
---

## Features & Capabilities

AI interviews, shortlisting, and coding — from one recruiter workspace.

### AI voice interview

Prabhat runs a live voice interview inside the meeting. It speaks the questions, listens to the candidate’s answers in real time (speech-to-speech) interview, and continues the conversation until the session is done. Candidates stay in Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, or Webex — no extra app

- Joins the same meeting the candidate already opened
- Familiar conferencing UI, so the interview feels like a normal call

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/ai-voice-interview.png`

---

### Recruiter dashboard

A single home for hiring ops: live and scheduled interviews, completed reports, and pass rates at a glance. Recruiters start a new screen without leaving the workspace.

- Live, scheduled, and completed counts in one view
- Jump straight into a new interview from the dashboard

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/recruiter-dashboard.png`

---

### Joins any meeting link

Paste the join URI from your scheduler. Prabhat enters that room as a voice participant. Teams, Zoom, Meet, and Webex are supported.

- Uses the direct join link from calendar or ATS
- Candidates keep the meeting they already know

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/joins-any-meeting-link.png`

---

### Job requirements

Capture the role once: job title, description, and requirements. Prabhat uses that JD to generate questions and score resumes against the same bar.

- One source of truth for the role
- Feeds both the voice plan and CV scoring

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/job-requirements.png`

---

### Multiple interview schedule

Schedule several interviews from one flow. Attach the JD, resume, meeting link, and optional coding round, then run them as a batch instead of one-off setup.

- Built for high-volume hiring days
- Each session still gets its own report

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/multiple-interview-schedule.png`

---

### Live interview monitoring

Watch bot status, active meetings, and session progress from the recruiter dashboard while the interview runs.

- See whether the bot has joined, is speaking, or is waiting
- Keep hiring ops in one place instead of hopping between meeting tools

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/live-interview-monitoring.png`

---

### Scored interview reports

Every session produces a hiring-ready report: per-question scores, strengths, gaps, and the full transcript for hiring managers.

- Consistent scoring across candidates for the same role
- Optional candidate feedback after the session

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/scored-interview-reports.png`

---

### Live coding assessment

Attach a timed coding round to the role. Candidates write and run code in the browser, with language choice and proctoring while they work.

- Results sit next to the voice interview report
- Proctoring flags keep the round accountable without a separate tool

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/live-coding-assessment.png`

---

### AI coding problem generation

Generate coding tasks from the role instead of maintaining a static bank only. Recruiters get problems that match the job, then attach them to the assessment.

- Role-aligned tasks, not generic puzzles
- Review before the candidate starts

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/ai-coding-problem-generation.png`

---

### Bulk JD upload

Upload many job descriptions at once when you are filling multiple roles. Prabhat creates the requirements without re-typing each JD.

- High-volume intake without one-by-one forms
- Pairs with bulk resume screening for the same campaign

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/bulk-jd-upload.png`

---

### Bulk resume screening

Upload or import resumes in bulk and score them against the job description so recruiters can shortlist before they schedule a voice interview.

- Rank candidates against the same JD
- Works with file upload or ATS import

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/bulk-resume-screening.png`

---

### Team management

Invite recruiters and viewers in your company, set roles, and keep access inside the organization.

- Org admin, recruiter, and viewer roles
- Access is granted by your team — not open public signup

**Screenshot:** `screenshots/team-management.png`
